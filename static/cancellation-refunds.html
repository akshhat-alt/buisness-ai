Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026

Refunds

No refunds are provided after payment has been processed. Please review the Service and its pricing carefully before subscribing.

Cancellation

Bizistic subscription payments are currently processed as individual charges rather than an automatically recurring subscription, so there is no recurring charge that needs to be cancelled in advance. If you wish to stop using Bizistic or close your account, contact support@bizistic.com and we will deactivate your account. This does not entitle you to a refund of any payment already made, per the policy above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@bizistic.com.
