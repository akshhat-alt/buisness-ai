Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Bizistic is operated by Akshat Wankhade, a sole proprietor based at 84, Vaishali Nagar, Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India. This Privacy Policy explains what information Bizistic collects and how it is used.

1. Information we collect

When you sign up and use Bizistic, we collect: your account details (name, email, business name); the business content and knowledge you upload; messages exchanged between your customers and the Bizistic assistant on WhatsApp and on your website; and staff/task information you enter for your own business operations.

2. How we use it

We use this information to operate the assistant for your business, generate the AI responses and business intelligence you see in your dashboard, send you account and billing emails, and process payments for your subscription.

3. Third parties we rely on

Bizistic is built on top of the following third-party services, each of which processes relevant data as part of delivering the Service:

  • OpenAI — processes conversation content to generate the assistant's AI responses.
  • Meta / WhatsApp Cloud API — used to send and receive WhatsApp messages on your behalf.
  • Razorpay — processes your subscription payments. Bizistic does not store your card or payment instrument details directly; these are handled by Razorpay.
  • Resend and Google Workspace — used to send transactional and account-related email.

4. Data storage and isolation

Each business's data is kept isolated from other businesses using Bizistic. Sensitive credentials (such as API keys you provide us) are stored encrypted, not in plain text.

5. Data retention and deletion

We retain your account and business data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting support@bizistic.com.

6. No sale of personal data

We do not sell your personal data or your customers' data to third parties.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting support@bizistic.com.

8. Children's privacy

Bizistic is intended for use by businesses and is not directed at children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to support@bizistic.com.
